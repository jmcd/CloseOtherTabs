# Chrome Web Store listing — Close Other Tabs

## Store listing tab

**Summary** (shown under the name; max 132 characters):
Keep the tab you're on and close every other tab in the window — one click or Alt+Shift+Q.

**Category:** Tools

**Description:**
Too many tabs open? Close Other Tabs keeps the one you're looking at and closes the rest of the window in one go.

• Click the toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+Q
• Only the current window is affected — other windows are left alone
• Pinned tabs stay open, just like Chrome's own "Close other tabs"
• No permissions, no data collected, no settings to fiddle with

Change the shortcut any time at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Closed a tab by mistake? Press Ctrl+Shift+T (Cmd+Shift+T on Mac) to bring it back.
